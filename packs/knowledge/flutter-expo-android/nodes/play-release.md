# Play release needs a checklist

A Play upload is a product event, not a zip of the last green build.

- Signing, track, and version must match the receipt
- Screenshots and listing copy come from the shipped build
- Do not invent Play Console state

Pair with `skills/toris-flutter-play-store-release/SKILL.md` when the app is Flutter.
