# Listing is part of the product

The store page is the product's handshake. Treat listing copy like UI copy: it must match the build that just shipped.

- Screenshot the paid path, not the settings screen
- Price and category are product decisions; record them in MEMORY.md when they change
- A listing rewrite without a ship is marketing fanfic
