# Never promise rankings

How we actually talk about growth here: describe the change, the evidence, and the uncertainty. "This title is more specific to the query" is allowed. "This will rank #1" is not. If a client or a future-self note wants a guarantee, rewrite it as a testable listing or on-page change instead.
