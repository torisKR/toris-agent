# Product growth

How a solo builder gets found and listed without a growth team.

Use this domain when the work is **discoverability** (search, generative engines, store listing) or **shipping a loop** that produces public evidence. Pair with the builtin skills `seo-geo-optimizer` and `app-store-listing-creator`. Read those SKILL.md files before changing a live site or store console.

## When to use

- Website launch, migration, or ranking / crawl review
- Store listing copy, screenshots, keyword mapping
- Closing a build → evidence → ship → list loop

## Anti-jobs

- Do not promise traffic, rankings, or featured answers
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, or expertise
- Do not mass-generate low-value pages for keyword variants
